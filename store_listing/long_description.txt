EmailKick helps service and support teams prepare clear customer email drafts without rebuilding the same job context for every request.

Move through a guided composer with Sender, Contacts, Coworker, Content, Actions, Information, and Help sections. Contacts combines company and department details, a recipient roster, and customer phone, shipping address, and machine records. Add each recipient's name, position, phone, and email, then assign them to To, CC, or BCC. Add multiple coworkers and choose which ones to CC from Content. Choose service modes to create an editable subject, while order numbers create an editable inbox preheader. When you are ready, EmailKick opens the prepared draft in your default mail app.

REUSE THE DETAILS THAT MATTER

Save sender profiles for your name, email, reply-to, and phone number. Save recipient profiles with company, department, and individual contact rows. Assign each individual to one recipient field—To, CC, or BCC—and edit or delete rows as needed. Save customer records with phone, shipping address, and machine records, including machine names, numbers, model names, and serial numbers. Add multiple coworker records with name, email, phone, and role. EmailKick automatically saves and restores the current draft and its contacts, selections, and field values on installed app builds.

MOVE DATA IN AND OUT

Import EmailKick data from JSON or CSV. Export saved data as JSON or CSV backups, or generate CSV templates for preparing reusable records. The app shows where exported files are stored and can open or copy the export location.

STAY IN CONTROL OF SENDING

EmailKick validates the required fields and opens a prepared draft in your default mail app with recipients, subject, and structured message content filled in. It never sends email automatically, so you can review every detail before sending.

Selected documents are added to the draft as file references. Attach the actual files in your mail app before sending.

KEY FEATURES

• Guided service-email workflow
• Reusable sender and recipient profiles, customer records, and machine records
• Recipient contact rows with name, position, phone, and email; assign each row to To, CC, or BCC
• Multiple editable coworkers, selectable for CC from the Content section
• Customer phone, shipping address, and associated machine records
• To, CC, and BCC email validation before opening a draft
• Editable generated subjects and preheaders
• Automatic local draft saving and restoration on installed app builds
• Web/PWA data is temporary for the current browser session; database persistence is unavailable
• JSON and CSV import and export
• Reusable CSV templates
• Responsive layouts for phone, tablet, and desktop screens
• Built-in help and support contact form
